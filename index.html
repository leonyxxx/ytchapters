ytchapters

Automatic timestamped chapter lists for YouTube live streams.

ytchapters is a self-hosted tool operated by a single channel owner. It watches one YouTube channel for new live broadcasts, waits until YouTube's auto-generated captions for that broadcast become available, compresses the transcript into a time-blocked summary, and uses a language model to pick the points where the topic changes. The resulting list of chapters is posted as a comment under the same video, on behalf of the channel owner who authorised the tool.

How it is used

The tool acts only on the authorising account's own videos. It reads publicly available video pages and caption tracks, and writes a single comment per broadcast through the YouTube Data API v3 (commentThreads.insert). It posts nothing else, edits nothing, and uploads nothing.

Privacy

No personal data is collected, stored, or shared with third parties. See the privacy policy.
